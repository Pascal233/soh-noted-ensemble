Our Craft

What We Do

From sacred oratorios to vibrant African arrangements, every note we sing is a celebration of who we are.

Choral Concerts

We mount full-scale choral concerts "” Christmas carols, Easter cantatas, and anniversary galas. Each production is meticulously rehearsed and staged for a memorable audience experience.

Sacred & Classical Music

Our repertoire includes Handel, Bach, Brahms and Mendelssohn, alongside Ghanaian and African sacred compositions performed with technical precision and deep reverence.

Event Performances

We perform at weddings, state functions, church anniversaries, corporate events and graduation ceremonies "” elevating any occasion with the power of united voices.

Vocal Training & Workshops

We run structured voice training sessions, sight-reading workshops and music theory classes open to members and the broader Kumasi community.

Soloists & Feature Acts

Our gifted soloists are available for lead roles in oratorios, special renditions and collaborative performances with orchestras and other musical groups.

Recordings & Productions

We produce studio and live recordings of our concerts and original arrangements "” preserving our performances and making our music accessible everywhere.

Who We Are

The Voice of Kumasi

Soh NOTED Ensemble is a premier choral group based in Kumasi, the Ashanti Regional capital of Ghana. We were founded with one purpose "” to bring people together through the power of exceptional choral music.

From Christmas carols that fill cathedrals to Easter cantatas that move congregations to tears, we pour everything into every performance. Our music spans classical oratorios, Ghanaian and African sacred compositions, gospel anthems and contemporary arrangements.

We are more than a choir. We are a family, a movement, and a voice for the culture of Kumasi "” and we sing from the heart every single time.

Ghanaian Heritage

Proudly celebrating our cultural identity through music.

Every performance crafted to move hearts and uplift spirits.

The People

Meet Our Team

The hearts and voices behind Soh NOTED Ensemble.

Executive Director Composer

Frederick Owusu Boakye

"Moxart"

Frederick Owusu Boakye is the Executive Director and a gifted composer at the heart of Soh NOTED Ensemble. His visionary leadership has shaped the ensemble into the premier choral group it is today, guiding its artistic direction and institutional growth since the group's founding in Kumasi.

As a composer, Frederick brings a unique voice to Ghanaian choral music "” blending sacred tradition, African heritage and contemporary expression into original works that have become beloved by audiences across the Ashanti Region. His compositions carry the DNA of the ensemble: disciplined, soulful and deeply rooted in the culture of Ghana.

Beyond directing and composing, he is a passionate advocate for choral music education, mentoring young voices and building the next generation of choristers in Kumasi and beyond.
