Our Craft

What We Do

From sacred oratorios to vibrant African arrangements, every note we sing is an act of dedication to the art of choral music.

Choral Concerts

We mount full-scale choral concerts — Christmas carols, Easter cantatas, and anniversary galas. Each production is meticulously rehearsed and staged for a memorable audience experience.

Sacred & Classical Music

Our repertoire includes Handel, Bach, Brahms and Mendelssohn, alongside Ghanaian and African sacred compositions performed with technical precision and deep reverence.

Event Performances

We perform at weddings, state functions, church anniversaries, corporate events and graduation ceremonies — elevating any occasion with the power of united voices.

Vocal Training & Workshops

We run structured voice training sessions, sight-reading workshops and music theory classes open to members and the broader Kumasi community.

Soloists & Feature Acts

Our gifted soloists are available for lead roles in oratorios, special renditions and collaborative performances with orchestras and other musical groups.

Recordings & Productions

We produce studio and live recordings of our concerts and original arrangements — preserving our performances and making our music accessible everywhere.

Who We Are

The Voice of Kumasi

Soh NOTED Ensemble is a premier choral group based in Kumasi, the Ashanti Regional capital of Ghana. Founded with a passionate commitment to excellence and a deep love for choral music, we have grown into one of the most celebrated vocal ensembles in the region.

Our repertoire spans classical oratorios, African choral compositions, gospel anthems and contemporary arrangements — delivered with precision, emotion and unity. Whether performing at cathedrals, stadiums or intimate venues, we bring audiences to their feet.

We are more than a choir. We are a family bound by music, culture and the shared conviction that harmony has the power to transform lives.
